## One-line pitch (for judges)

While US equities sleep, VIGIL reads closed-market news, refuses by default, and papers **one** allowlisted Bitget Demo trade only when the window, the name, and the evidence agree — with a full why-log.

---

## Role of the LLM (paste)

VIGIL scores closed-market events through a multi-path LLM gateway: **AgentRouter** primary (`deepseek-v4-flash`, Tor egress when required), with optional Venice / DashScope failover. Provider+model are declared on every why-card. FENN empty-allowlist refuses without calling the LLM. The LLM never places live orders; Bitget Demo UTA paper (`paptrading:1`) executes simulated fills only.

## Project description — six parts (paste)

**1. Thesis**  
While US RTH is closed, Bitget tokenized US stocks still move on news. VIGIL only activates in that closed window, checks observed move vs headline, then papers one allowlisted name with a full why-log. Most headlines become NO cards on purpose (FENN refuse-by-default). A weekend gap alone is not a fill.

**2. Target user**  
Crypto-native traders watching Bitget tokenized US equities who cannot babysit screens all weekend. They need an explainable after-hours paper co-pilot — not a 24/7 spam bot.

**3. Validation**  
Paper trading log from the competition window (timestamp, pair, side, qty, status, exchange orderId, why-hash). Metrics labeled observed / estimated / targeted. We do not invent closed Sharpe from open Demo buys; accepted Demo fills + refuse rates are the honest scoreboard.

**4. Progress**  
Production TanStack Start UI, multitenant cookie sessions, Neon Postgres, TinyFish news, Tor LLM path, Bitget Demo paper executor, $100→$5k growth allowlist (NVDA/AMD/AAPL/TSLA), Desk chat with hard daily cap, terminal playbooks, append-only why-log.

**5. Deliverables**  
- Live demo: https://vigil-one-wine.vercel.app  
- GitHub: https://github.com/henrysammarfo/vigil  
- Paper log: Dashboard → Paper trades → Export (`vigil-paper-log-*.json`)  
- Demo video: `docs/submission/artifacts/vigil-s2-demo.mp4`  
- This pack + why-log replay

**6. AI Trading take**  
Agents win when the window is constrained and evidence is inspectable. Mute latency models race milliseconds; VIGIL optimizes trust and closed-market edge on Agent Hub paper.
